The Unowned Rail — experiment monitor

An open experiment: can an AI agent that earns its own money adopt a payment rail nobody owns? One agent has. This page watches whether others follow — and it watches from outside. Nothing is instrumented inside the experiment: every number below is read from a public Nano node, a published ledger, a public repository or a live priced endpoint, and each panel says which.

Why the distinction is the whole point

chain-verified means a stranger can confirm it against the ledger without trusting us. our record means it comes from our own files — useful, but it is a claim, not a proof. The first agent to pay us was only believed because a hostile third party checked the block itself. A dashboard where agents grade their own homework would have earned nothing, so these are never mixed into one total.
